# VALIDATION
# Version 1.0
# ==========================================================

## Purpose

Validation is the final quality assurance stage before execution.

Its purpose is to verify that every creative decision remains consistent with the established world.

No final image should be considered complete until validation has been performed.

---

## Validation Principle

Validation does not evaluate aesthetics alone.

It evaluates consistency.

A visually attractive image is not considered successful if it contradicts the established world, physical logic or project objective.

---

## World Consistency

Verify that:

- The environment remains consistent.
- Scene Locks have not changed unexpectedly.
- Variable Assets have not unintentionally modified the world.
- The visual identity remains consistent.

---

## Physical Logic

Verify that:

- Gravity behaves naturally.
- Object interactions are believable.
- Heat, liquid and steam behave logically.
- Contact between objects follows physical rules.

---

## Material Behaviour

Verify that:

- Materials respond correctly to light.
- Surface roughness is believable.
- Transparency and reflections are consistent.
- Wet and dry materials behave differently.
- Material properties remain internally consistent.

---

## Lighting

Verify that:

- Light direction is consistent.
- Shadows match the light source.
- Reflections match the surrounding environment.
- Colour temperature is coherent.
- Exposure supports the creative objective.

---

## Composition

Verify that:

- Visual hierarchy is clear.
- The Hero Subject remains dominant.
- Balance and negative space support communication.
- Perspective remains consistent.
- Reading flow supports the intended message.

---

## Commercial Evaluation

Verify that:

- The communication objective is clear.
- Brand value is supported.
- The product remains the primary focus.
- Attention is directed appropriately.
- The image is commercially usable.

---

## AI Artifact Detection

Actively identify and reduce:

- Plastic-looking materials
- Repetitive object patterns
- Uniform distribution
- Impossible geometry
- Overly perfect symmetry
- Unrealistic reflections
- Incorrect perspective
- Floating objects
- Texture repetition
- Artificial-looking steam
- Unrealistic food appearance

---

## Retouch Validation

When performing image retouching, verify that:

- Existing composition has been preserved unless intentionally changed.
- Scene Locks remain unchanged.
- Improvements do not introduce new inconsistencies.
- Local modifications do not affect unrelated elements.

---

## Final Decision

Before completing the task, confirm:

✓ World remains believable.

✓ Physical logic is consistent.

✓ Materials behave naturally.

✓ Lighting supports the world.

✓ Composition supports communication.

✓ Commercial objective has been achieved.

✓ No significant AI artifacts remain.

Only after all validation stages have passed should the final result be delivered.
