# WORLD SIMULATION WORKFLOW

## Step 1 — Understand Intent

Identify:

- Project objective
- Communication goal
- Target audience
- Brand
- Deliverable
- Platform

Question:

What is this image trying to achieve?

---

## Step 2 — Build the World

Imagine the environment before the camera exists.

Questions:

- Where is this?
- What time?
- Indoor or outdoor?
- What atmosphere?
- What story is happening?

---

## Step 3 — Physics

Define physical rules.

Examples:

- Gravity
- Weight
- Balance
- Heat
- Wind
- Liquid flow

Question:

How does this world physically work?

---

## Step 4 — Materials

Identify every important material.

Examples:

- Metal
- Glass
- Food
- Wood
- Fabric
- Plastic

Question:

How should each material react to light and surrounding objects?

---

## Step 5 — Behaviour

Simulate interactions.

Examples:

- Steam rises
- Sauce flows
- Oil reflects
- Ice melts
- Food compresses
- Paper wrinkles

Question:

How do objects interact naturally?

---

## Step 6 — Lighting

Determine:

- Light source
- Direction
- Intensity
- Color temperature
- Shadow behaviour

Question:

Why does this scene have this lighting?

---

## Step 7 — Camera

Determine:

- Lens
- Camera angle
- Focus
- Depth of field
- Perspective

Question:

Why was this camera chosen?

---

## Step 8 — Composition

Determine:

- Visual hierarchy
- Balance
- Negative space
- Reading direction
- Product emphasis

Question:

Does the composition support communication?

---

## Step 9 — Commercial QA

Check:

- Is attention captured?
- Is the product clear?
- Is the message understandable?
- Is the brand supported?
- Is it commercially believable?

---

## Step 10 — Generate Prompt

Only after every previous step is complete.

The prompt should describe the simulated world, not merely list visual objects.
