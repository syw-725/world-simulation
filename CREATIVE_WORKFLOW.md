# CREATIVE WORKFLOW
Version: 0.1

## Purpose

This workflow defines how every image generation project should be approached.

Never generate an image immediately.

Always understand the problem, simulate the world, and validate the scene before writing the final prompt.

The objective is to create believable commercial visuals instead of simply generating aesthetically pleasing images.

---

# Workflow

## Step 1 — Understand the Brief

Identify:

- Project objective
- Brand
- Target audience
- Communication goal
- Platform
- Output format
- Constraints

Question:

What problem is this visual trying to solve?

Output:

A clear creative objective.

---

## Step 2 — Understand the Intent

Identify the true purpose behind the brief.

Examples:

- Attract attention
- Create memory
- Trigger emotion
- Communicate information
- Build brand value
- Sell the product

Question:

What should the audience think, feel and do after seeing this image?

Output:

Communication intent.

---

## Step 3 — Build the World

Before describing the image, construct the world.

Determine:

- Environment
- Time
- Weather
- Space
- Story
- Atmosphere

Question:

If this scene really exists, what kind of world is it?

Output:

Complete world description.

---

## Step 4 — Physics

Apply real-world logic.

Examples:

- Gravity
- Weight
- Balance
- Heat
- Liquid flow
- Steam
- Wind

Question:

How does this world physically behave?

Output:

Physical rules.

---

## Step 5 — Materials

Identify every important material.

Examples:

- Food
- Metal
- Glass
- Fabric
- Wood
- Plastic
- Stone

Question:

How should every material react?

Output:

Material properties.

---

## Step 6 — Behaviour

Simulate natural interaction.

Examples:

- Food settles naturally
- Oil reflects correctly
- Steam rises
- Ice melts
- Wrinkles appear
- Shadows respond naturally

Question:

How do all objects interact with each other?

Output:

Behaviour simulation.

---

## Step 7 — Lighting

Determine:

- Main light
- Fill light
- Shadow
- Reflection
- Color temperature

Question:

Why does this scene have this lighting?

Output:

Lighting design.

---

## Step 8 — Camera

Determine:

- Lens
- Perspective
- Camera angle
- Focus
- Depth of field

Question:

Why was this camera chosen?

Output:

Camera language.

---

## Step 9 — Composition

Determine:

- Visual hierarchy
- Reading flow
- Product emphasis
- Balance
- Negative space

Question:

Does the composition support communication?

Output:

Composition strategy.

---

## Step 10 — Quality Assurance

Validate:

- World is believable
- Physics is consistent
- Materials are logical
- Behaviour is natural
- Lighting is coherent
- Camera supports communication
- Composition supports communication
- Commercial objective is achieved

Question:

Would a human believe this scene?

Output:

QA report.

---

## Step 11 — Prompt Generation

Only after all previous steps are complete.

The prompt should describe the simulated world rather than simply list visual objects.

The prompt is the result of reasoning, not the starting point.
