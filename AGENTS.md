## Project instructions

Before starting, planning, generating, reviewing, revising, or delivering any
video-production work in this project, read `VIDEO_PRODUCTION_SPEC.md` in full
and follow its stage gates, file/version rules, cost controls, and final QC
checklist.

Treat the specification as a workflow standard only. Never carry a previous
project's style, scene, character, environment, product, prop, palette, camera
language, or model choice into a new project unless the user explicitly asks.
Create and approve a project-specific creative pack for every new video.

The user must explicitly double-check and approve the complete storyboard
before any video-generation job is submitted.

Do not submit paid or credit-consuming generations until the required creative,
asset, animatic, and cost gates in that specification are satisfied or the user
explicitly approves an exception.
